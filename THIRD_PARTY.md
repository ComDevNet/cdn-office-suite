# Upstream apps and icons

The bundled web archives are distributed without changing their application code. Both upstream MIT and Apache-2.0 license files are retained in every installed release.

Official app icons are used unmodified from each bundled release’s assets/app-icon directory:

- https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft/tree/v0.5.0/assets/app-icon
- https://github.com/storytold/gridcraft/tree/v0.5.0/assets/app-icon
- https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft/tree/v0.5.0/assets/app-icon

The app icons use upstream MIT OR Apache-2.0 licensing. Upstream trademarks and wordmarks are separate. Icon sources, hashes, release URLs and archive hashes are in deckcraft/bundled/releases.json. The existing CDN logo and mail artwork are retained from this repository.
