# CDN Office Suite

Audience: students and staff on shared OC4D servers.
Purpose: launch the seven installed Craft tools and existing mail. Superadmins check, review and approve individual Craft releases in a separate Updates tab.
Personality: friendly and playful, as requested by the user; no sidebar.
Constraints: offline local assets; browser-based editing; device-local files; preserve old documents and mail; truthful availability; no automatic updates; signed OC4D login and database role checks.
Accessibility: keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts.
